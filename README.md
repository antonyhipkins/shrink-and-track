# Shrink & Track

A weight loss, calorie and body measurement tracker that runs in your web browser.

Track weight (kg, lb or stones), calories and macros, body measurements, BMI and target weight, activity, water and habits. It also has a week planner, a shopping list and an optional personal medication log.

Everything is stored in your own browser on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Shrink & Track is a personal tracking tool. It is not medical advice and not a medical device.

For personal use only. Please do not copy, resell or redistribute.
